# SACH — a truth check at the pay screen

Python · LightGBM · Kotlin · JavaScript · Team Koshish, four people

Before a risky payment goes through, SACH asks who told the payer to pay, checks the answer against facts about the payee and warns on a mismatch, never blocking. Mismatches, with no message content, feed a radar for new scam waves. Built for the RAKSHAM AI Cybersecurity Hackathon at IIT Delhi; I owned the engine and the ML.

Grand Finalist (Top 30), October 2026. A hackathon prototype on synthetic and public data only.
